Privacy Policy — Students-Ecosystem

Last updated: [DATE]

Who we are

Students-Ecosystem is an educational planning platform helping international students research universities, degree programs, visa requirements, and living costs. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights regarding it.

What we collect

  • Account information you provide: full name, email, password (stored as a secure hash — we never store or have access to your plain-text password)
  • Profile information you choose to add: nationality, academic results (CGPA/percentage), available funds — used only to match you with relevant programs and calculate estimated costs. This is never shared with universities, partners, or any third party without your explicit action.
  • Application and document tracking data you enter: application milestones, document expiry dates and notes (we do not store uploaded files — only dates, types, and notes you enter yourself)
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, and clicks on partner links (used to improve the platform and, where applicable, to track affiliate referrals)

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or any third party
  • We do not share your profile or application data with universities, partners, or anyone else without your explicit action (for example, choosing to submit a professor recommendation request, or clicking through to a partner service)
  • We do not use your data to train external AI models or share it with data brokers

Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

For users in the EU/EEA, we process your data based on: your consent (where you actively provide information), the necessity of processing to provide the service you've requested (a contract-like basis), and our legitimate interest in improving the platform (e.g. anonymized usage analytics).

Your rights

  • Access: you can view and export all data we hold about you at any time via "Export my data"
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and all associated data at any time via "Delete my account"
  • Correction: you can update your profile information at any time
  • Portability: your exported data is provided in a structured, machine-readable format
  • If you are located in the EU/EEA, you have the rights described above under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), plus the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority
  • To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [YOUR EMAIL]

Affiliate links and partner services

Some links on this platform, shown in the "Trusted Partners" section, are affiliate/referral links. We may earn a commission if you choose to use a partner service, at no additional cost to you. We only list services we believe are genuinely useful to international students. Using a partner service is always optional and has no effect on your access to the rest of the platform.

Data storage and security

  • Your data is stored with [YOUR HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Render], using encryption in transit (TLS/SSL) for all connections
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no system can guarantee absolute security

International data transfers

Because this platform serves students internationally, your data may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence. Where this involves transferring data from the EU/EEA to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards as required by GDPR (such as standard contractual clauses with our hosting provider).
